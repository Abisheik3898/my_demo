✅ Project Title: Automated Bird’s Food Feeding System


🛠️ Project Description: The Automated Bird’s Food Feeding System is a smart device designed to automatically dispense bird food at regular intervals, ensuring that birds are fed even when the caretaker is unavailable. The project combines sensor technology, microcontroller programming, and mechanical design to create a hands-free, reliable feeding mechanism.

The system uses a microcontroller (e.g., Arduino) to control a servo motor or DC motor that opens the food container at scheduled times. An ultrasonic sensor monitors the food level, and an optional LCD display or alert system can notify when refilling is needed. This project promotes wildlife care and is especially useful in urban or remote settings.

🔧 Key Features:
⇨ Timer-based feeding mechanism (e.g., every 6 or 12 hours)
⇨ Motorized dispenser controlled by Arduino or other microcontroller
⇨ Food level monitoring using sensors
⇨ Low-power operation, suitable for solar-powered systems
⇨ Customizable design for different bird types or quantities

✅ Project Title: Characterization on Habara Fiber


🧪 Project Description: This project focuses on the characterization and analysis of Habara fiber, a natural fiber with potential applications in textiles, composites, and eco-friendly materials. The objective was to study its physical, chemical, thermal, and mechanical properties to evaluate its suitability as a sustainable alternative to synthetic fibers.
The fiber was extracted, treated, and subjected to various tests such as tensile strength, moisture absorption, Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and thermal gravimetric analysis (TGA). These tests helped in understanding the fiber’s structure, bonding, durability, and thermal stability.
🔬 Key Activities:
Extraction and processing of raw Habara fiber
Mechanical testing (e.g., tensile strength, elongation)
Thermal and chemical analysis using:
FTIR (Functional groups)
TGA (Thermal degradation point)
SEM (Surface morphology)
Comparison with other natural and synthetic fibers
